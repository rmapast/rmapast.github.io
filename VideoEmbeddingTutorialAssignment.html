Video Embedding Tutorial for HTML5

First, you need to find a video that you want to embed in your page.

Then you are going to open the video and click the share button which is going to open a window.

In the window there are quite a few options and you need to click the Embed option.

And now you are going to click the copy button. this will copy the HTML tag to the clipboard.

Now you don't need the window and you can press the X button at the top right corner of the window.

Now you paste your code into your HTML file using any text editor.

Now you have an embedded video like this one.
